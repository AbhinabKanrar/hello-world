# Eurobarometer Survey Results: Sport and Physical Activity (2026)

## Engaging in Physical Activity
* **Exercising several times a week:** 38% (up 6 percentage points from 2022)
* **Exercising almost daily:** 9% (up 3 percentage points from 2022)
* **Never exercise or play sport:** 39% (decreased from 45% in 2022)

## Venues for Exercise
* **Parks and outdoor spaces:** 47% (stable from 2022)
* **Health and fitness facilities:** 39% (up from 33% in 2022)
* **Active commuting:** 37% (up from 24% in 2022)
* **Walking for at least 10 minutes at a time:** 72% (up from 16% in 2022)

## Motivations (Mental Health & Wellbeing)
* **Improving or maintaining physical health:** 44%
* **Relaxing:** 39%
* **Improving physical performance:** 30%
* **Travelling from one place to another:** 30%
* **Improving mental health or reducing stress:** 26%
